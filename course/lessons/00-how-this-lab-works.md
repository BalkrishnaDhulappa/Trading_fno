# 00 — How this lab works

This is a **personal, offline** chart lab. It follows Vishal B. Malkan’s **3×3 swing** spine: candles, price structure, RSI, Bollinger — on **three timeframes**.

It is **not** a live terminal and **not** an iPhone app (that can come later).

## The 3×3 idea

Three tools:

- **Candlesticks** (with volume when it matters)
- **Price structure** (support, resistance, swings)
- **Indicators** — RSI (40/60) and Bollinger (dual band / father–son)

Three timeframes (family names he uses):

- **Grandfather = monthly**
- **Father = weekly**
- **Son = daily**

You read **top down**. The son does not argue with grandfather.

## How you study (every sitting)

1. Read **one** lesson PDF until the idea is boringly clear.
2. Open the **chart pack** PDF (same sitting or next day).
3. For each stock, mark **M / W / D** using only what that lesson taught.
4. Write a one-line verdict. Skip is a valid answer.

He says look at many charts. Start with **the pack you have on the iPad**, not 100 on day one. Increase count when the marks are fast and consistent.

## RSI lines in this lab

Charts are drawn with **RSI 14** and horizontal **40** and **60** — not 30/70.

- **Bullish range:** RSI mostly **40–80**, dips hold ~40
- **Neutral:** RSI chopping **40–60**
- **Bearish range:** RSI mostly **20–60**, rallies fail ~60

**PRD / NRD = divergence** (your definition):

- **PRD:** price **lower low**, RSI **higher low**
- **NRD:** price **higher high**, RSI **lower high**

## What these files are

- `lessons/` — concepts in 3×3 order
- `checklists/annotation.md` — the mark-up sheet
- Chart pack PDF — **historical** candles, not live

Copy the PDFs onto the iPad (**Files**, or import into Goodnotes / Notability / Books). Airplane mode after that.

Not brokerage advice. Not a copy of his paid classroom word-for-word. If a 3×3 video and this PDF disagree, **his video wins** — annotate the PDF.
