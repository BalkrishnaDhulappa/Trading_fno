# 04 — Range shift

A **range shift** is a **change of character**, not one spike through 40 or 60.

## Bullish shift (bearish box → bullish box)

Typical story:

- RSI had been capped near **60**, living in the **bearish** box.
- Then it **starts holding 40** on dips (no new collapse into the 20s as a habit).
- Then it **accepts 60** — closes/holds above, uses 60 as support later.

You are watching **acceptance**, not a wick.

## Bearish shift (bullish box → bearish box)

Typical story:

- RSI had been holding **40** in a **bullish** box.
- Then **60 fails** on strength (not a new 70s walk).
- Then **40 breaks** and RSI cannot reclaim it cleanly — bearish box **20–60**.

## Neutral is the hallway

Many shifts **pass through 40–60 chop**. Chop is not the new trend. Wait until one box is the **new home**.

## 3×3 use

Range shift on **weekly or monthly** is a big deal. Daily shift inside an unchanged weekly range is often just the son making noise.

Mark shift **where it started** (approximate bar cluster), not a fantasy exact tick.

## Practice on the chart pack

On each timeframe: **no shift / bullish shift / bearish shift** in the visible history.  
Circle the cluster. If nothing, write **stable range**.
