# 02 — Price structure (3×3)

Second tool: **where** the candle sits. Same hammer at the middle of nowhere is junk. Same hammer at **demand** is the 3×3 point.

## Swings

- **Swing high:** a high with lower highs around it. Resistance candidate.
- **Swing low:** a low with higher lows around it. Support candidate.

On **monthly**, swings are slow and expensive to be wrong. On **daily**, swings are for execution.

## Support and resistance (zones, not magic prices)

Mark **zones**: a band of prices that held more than once. 3×3 finale talks S/R in real time — you practise on **static** charts.

- Support: buyers previously appeared.
- Resistance: sellers previously appeared.
- Broken resistance can become support (and the reverse).

## Trend vs range (price, not RSI yet)

- **Uptrend:** higher highs and higher lows.
- **Downtrend:** lower highs and lower lows.
- **Range:** highs and lows repeating in a box.

If price is in a box, your job this lesson is to **draw the box**, not to force a breakout story.

## Multi-timeframe structure (family)

- Monthly zone = **grandfather’s house**. Daily noise inside it is normal.
- Weekly often shows the **usable** swing for positional/swing holds.
- Daily shows the **door** (entry).

If daily support sits **inside** a monthly supply zone, that is conflict. Skip or wait. 3×3 is alignment, not heroics.

## Practice on the chart pack

On **monthly**: two zones max (major support, major resistance).  
On **weekly**: current swing high and swing low.  
On **daily**: is price at a zone, in the middle, or breaking?

One sentence: “Daily is at weekly support / mid-range / resistance.”

Still no full RSI essay. You may **glance** whether the zone lines up with an RSI 40/60 event — optional.
