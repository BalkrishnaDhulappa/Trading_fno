# 07 — Bollinger bands (dual band & father–son)

Last 3×3 indicator family: **volatility + trend**, still on two/three clocks.

## Default picture

Middle band = 20-period average. Outer bands = typical **2 standard deviations**. Price **walking the upper band** = strong uptrend (not an automatic short). Price hugging the lower band = strong downtrend.

**Squeeze** (bands narrow) = energy coiling. **Expansion** = the move.

## Dual band / volatility divergence (3×3 middle episodes)

He pairs **two band views** (settings or timeframes) so **volatility disagrees** with a sleepy average — a warning that range is about to change. In this lab, until we add a second band overlay on the pack, you **mark by eye**:

- Are bands **narrow** then **ripping**?
- Is the **walk** along one band intact or failing (close back inside, then opposite band)?

If the printed pack has no extra BB overlay yet, use **wicks vs a mental 20-bar middle** and wait for the next pack version. **Do not fake precise %B numbers.**

## Father–son (episode 11 in the public numbering)

- **Father** = higher TF (often weekly): **direction**  
- **Son** = lower TF (often daily): **entry**  
- Golden ratio talk: daily vs weekly ~**1:5**; weekly vs monthly ~**1:4**; keep father/son **4–6×** apart  

Example: weekly riding **upper** band (father up). Daily tags **mid or lower** band / support (son dip). That is a **buy-the-dip in father’s trend**, same spirit as RSI GFS, different tool.

Sell mirror: father on lower band walk; son pops to mid/upper into resistance.

## Combine with RSI (3×3 finale energy)

Best pages in your pack:

- Father trend **and** weekly RSI range agree  
- Son dip **and** daily RSI ~40 (long) or ~60 (short)  
- Candle + S/R confirm  

If BB says up and RSI weekly is a bearish box, **skip**. Alignment is the product.

## Practice on the chart pack

Without a BB overlay: still write **father trend** (week: rising / falling / box) and **son position** (high / mid / low of recent range).  
When overlays exist: mark upper-walk / lower-walk / squeeze.

Then one combined line: “GFS? BB father-son? both? neither?”
