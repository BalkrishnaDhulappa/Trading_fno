# 06 — GFS (grandfather–father–son)

RSI on **three** timeframes. Default swing set: **monthly / weekly / daily**.

## Bullish GFS (the 5-star sketch)

1. **Monthly RSI > 60** — grandfather bullish  
2. **Weekly RSI > 60** — father bullish  
3. **Daily RSI back near 40** (about 39–43 in scanner talk) — son is the dip  
4. **Alert:** daily green candle at that inflection, ideally at support  
5. **Idea:** buy strength above that candle’s high; stop under its low  
6. **First target language:** swing resistance and/or RSI back toward **60** on daily  

If monthly and weekly are **not** both strong, it is **not** this setup. Skip.

## Bearish mirror

1. Monthly RSI **< 40**  
2. Weekly RSI **< 40**  
3. Daily RSI rally near **60**  
4. Bearish alert candle  
5. Weakness below that candle’s low; stop above its high  

## Other timeframe triplets (same law)

He allows the family on other clocks if the **ratio is ~4–6×**:

- Weekly / daily / hourly (swing)  
- Daily / hourly / 15m (intraday)  

This **offline pack is M/W/D only**. Don’t invent hourly.

## Practice on the chart pack

Tick: M>60? W>60? D near 40? If yes, is there an alert candle?  
Or the short mirror.  
Most names will be **no setup**. That is the point of looking at many charts.
