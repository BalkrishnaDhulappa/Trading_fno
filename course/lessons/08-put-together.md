# 08 — Put it together + daily drill

3×3 wrap-up: candles, S/R, RSI range/shift/divergence, BB father–son, on **M/W/D**.

## Order of marks (same every stock)

1. **Structure** — zones, trend or box (M then W then D)  
2. **RSI range** — bull / neutral / bear on each  
3. **Shift** — yes/no  
4. **PRD / NRD** — strict divergence or none  
5. **GFS** — setup or not  
6. **BB father–son** — agree or not  
7. **Candle** — alert bar only if 5 or 6 is alive  
8. **Verdict** — long idea / short idea / skip  

If 8 is skip, you still **did the work**. That is the 100-chart habit.

## Daily drill (offline)

Use a new chart-pack PDF when you have one. Same universe is fine; **new dates** appear as history grows when you regenerate on a computer.

Suggested volume:

- Week 1: **5 names** × 3 charts, full checklist  
- Week 2–3: **10 names**  
- Later: **20+** toward his “many charts a day” — speed without sloppy divergence

## iPad

Import PDFs into **Goodnotes / Notability / Apple Books / Files**. Markup with Pencil. **No network** after the files are on the device.
