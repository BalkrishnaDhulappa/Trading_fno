# 01 — Candlesticks (3×3)

First tool in the 3×3 mix: **the candle**. Indicators wait. Structure waits. The bar tells you who won **that** period.

## Anatomy (you must see this without thinking)

- **Body:** open to close. Green/white-ish = close > open. Red = close < open.
- **Wicks:** rejected prices. Long lower wick = buyers showed up. Long upper wick = sellers showed up.
- **Range:** high minus low. Wide range = decision. Narrow range = indecision.

A pretty pattern with a **tiny range** at the wrong place is noise.

## Patterns he actually leans on in 3×3 wrap-ups

You do not need 50 Japanese names. You need a few **at the right address**.

### Hammer / bullish pin (at support or RSI ~40)

Small body near the **high**, long **lower** wick. Sellers pushed down; buyers took it back. Useful when **daily RSI is near 40** in a bullish higher-timeframe story.

### Shooting star / bearish pin (at resistance or RSI ~60)

Small body near the **low**, long **upper** wick. Useful when **daily RSI is near 60** in a bearish higher-timeframe story.

### Spinning top / doji

Small body, wicks both sides. **Pause**, not a signal. 3×3 wrap-up: spinning tops need **volume** and **level**. Alone they are “wait.”

### Engulfing

This bar’s body fully covers the previous body. Bullish engulfing after a dip into support / RSI 40. Bearish engulfing after a pop into resistance / RSI 60.

## Volume (3×3 reminder)

A hammer on **dead volume** is a maybe. A hammer on **rising volume** at a known support is an alert candle.

You will not always have volume on monthly charts in the pack. If volume is missing, **do not invent it**. Use wick + level + RSI only.

## Alert candle (GFS language, early)

In the RSI GFS buy: monthly and weekly already strong; daily RSI comes to ~40; you wait for a **green alert candle**. Practical mark:

- Note the **high** of that candle (break = entry idea)
- Note the **low** (invalid if broken)

You will use this again in the GFS lesson. For now, just **circle** such candles on daily charts when they sit on a level.

## Practice on the chart pack (this lesson only)

For each stock, daily chart first (son), then glance week/month:

- Circle 2–3 **decision** candles (hammer, star, engulfing, spinning top).
- Write whether volume helped, or “volume n/a”.
- Ignore RSI ranges except: *is this candle near a 40 or 60 touch?* Optional note only.

Do **not** call PRD/NRD yet. One tool.
