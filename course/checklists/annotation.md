# Chart annotation checklist (use after the concept is clear)

For every stock, mark **month**, **week**, and **day**.

## RSI 40 / 60
- [ ] Is RSI mostly 40–80 (bullish), 40–60 (neutral), or 20–60 (bearish)?
- [ ] Last touch of 40 or 60: held or failed?

## Range / shift
- [ ] Same range for 20+ bars, or a **range shift** in progress?

## PRD / NRD (divergence)
- [ ] PRD: price lower low, RSI higher low?
- [ ] NRD: price higher high, RSI lower high?
- [ ] Which timeframe? (M / W / D)

## GFS (after that lesson)
- [ ] Monthly RSI vs 60
- [ ] Weekly RSI vs 60
- [ ] Daily RSI vs 40 (long) or 60 (short)

## Candles / structure (after those lessons)
- [ ] Alert candle at the inflection?
- [ ] Nearby support / resistance?

## One-line verdict
Bullish / bearish / skip — because: ________
